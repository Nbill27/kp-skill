## Prompt

Saya magang di sebuah toko online, badannya CV, divisinya admin sosial media.
Prodi saya Sistem Informasi. Apakah topik "Analisis Pemasaran Digital" bisa
dipakai untuk KP?

## Why this case exists

Placement legality and program fit are hard requirements in section 3 and
section 9. A CV is acceptable, but the division has to support an SI analysis
of a business process or an IT audit. The skill must ask what the student
actually did before it endorses a topic, and must never promise approval.
