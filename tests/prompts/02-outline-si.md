## Prompt

Buatkan kerangka laporan KP saya. Prodi Sistem Informasi, magang di bagian
pelayanan pelanggan sebuah perusahaan logistik.

## Why this case exists

Section 9 forbids forcing one program's outline onto another. The outline is
per-program, and the method follows the problem. A generic five-chapter
template borrowed from skripsi is a failure even when it looks plausible.
