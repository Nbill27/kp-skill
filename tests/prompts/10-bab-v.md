## Prompt

Tulis kesimpulan dan saran. Di Bab IV saya cuma menganalisis proses dan
mengusulkan perbaikan, belum membuat aplikasinya.

## Why this case exists

Section 3 limits KP scope to analysis and recommendation unless evidence proves
otherwise, and section 8 requires every problem to map through to the
conclusion. A conclusion that reports a deployed system would be fabricated.
