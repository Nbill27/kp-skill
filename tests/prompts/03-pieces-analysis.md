## Prompt

Tolong isi analisis PIECES untuk sistem absensi di tempat magang saya. Saya
belum wawancara siapa pun, baru lihat aplikasinya sekali.

## Why this case exists

Section 10 requires every PIECES dimension to carry an observed condition, its
evidence, and its impact. With almost no evidence, the correct answer is a
mostly empty matrix plus a fieldwork plan, not six paragraphs of invented
friction.
