# Content Guide

## Core map

- Fill the core map before building the outline. If the should and the is do not differ, there is no document yet. Ask the student for a fact from the field or propose two candidate gaps marked [CEK: ...].
- Should: the theory, norm, regulation, or target the work must meet.
- Is: the field facts and data from the host company, for example the running process and its observed problems.
- Gap: the KP problem, stated as the difference between should and is.
- Reader: the supervisor and the examiners.
- Core claim: the answer the KP analysis will test, written as one sentence a stranger understands. If the claim cannot be written in one sentence, the outline is not ready.
- Proof: the method and the data, for example observation, interview, and company documents, plus the proposed system design.
- Action: the recommendation and the proposed system improvement for the host.
- Check the map for orphans. Every question, claim, or promise in the outline must have a section that answers it and evidence that supports it. Every section must serve one of them.
- Write the title last, after the outline and the check pass. If the check fails only because the student data is missing, give the title options anyway but label each one sementara. If the check fails for a structural reason such as no gap or an orphan question, fix the outline first and give no title.

## Research site and sidang references

- Place the research at a company, a government institution, or a business that holds an establishment permit.
- Bring 10 journal papers to the sidang. Count 1 paper for the Bab I background, 4 papers for the related research, and 5 papers for Bab I, Bab II, and Bab III.
- Bring 8 books or ebooks cited in Bab I, Bab II, and Bab III.

## Latar Belakang Masalah

- State the facts of the problem at the company.
- Strengthen the background with at least 1 citation from a prior research journal.
- Check that the cited journal shows its journal name and its ISSN or volume number.

## Identifikasi Masalah

- List the facts of the main problems that the background reflects.
- State each problem as a statement that the research can answer.

## Batasan Masalah

- Limit the scope and the coverage of the problem discussed in Bab IV and Bab V.
- Leave room for the author or another student to continue the research as a review study.

## Rumusan Masalah

- State the core problem as a narrative in question sentences.
- Restate the core problem from the identification, completed with the statements from the scope limits.

## Tujuan dan Manfaat Penelitian

- Explain the specific targets the research wants to reach.
- Explain the benefit for science development or the practical benefit of the studied problem.
- Use clear and accurate wording that leaves no room for misinterpretation.

## Sistematika Penulisan

- Describe each chapter that the report presents, followed by a short explanation of the material in that chapter.

## Tinjauan Pustaka

- Use the newest sources from the last 5 years counted from the start of the research.
- Draw the sources from primary literature such as articles, journals, monographs, and other original writings.
- Show how the relevant research developed for the title or the theme and how it directs the solution.
- Present the review as a quantitative or qualitative description, a mathematical model, or another representative form.
- Cite quotations, excerpts, and paraphrases with the author, the year, and the page in parentheses.

## Penelitian Terkait

- Present research related to the studied problem from journals with an ISSN.
- Use only journals from the last 5 years counted from the start of the research.

## Objek Penelitian

- Describe the company history, its business activities, and other general explanations in the history section.
- Draw the organization structure of the company or the studied part. Show the position of the studied information system and the related organization units.
- Explain the authority and the responsibility of each organization element according to the structure.

## Tinjauan Umum

- Describe the general picture of the running system that serves as the research object.
- Add a short description of how the system runs.

## Deskripsi Sistem

- Describe the running system in full, covering every process it executes.
- Match the diagrams to the problem and consult the supervisor on the choice. Use a document flowmap or flowchart for data procedures that follow a sequence of steps. Use a topology or an architecture diagram for a computer network case. Use an IoT architecture diagram for an IoT case.

## Permasalahan Sistem

- Analyze the problem in four stages. Understand the problem area, analyze the problem and the opportunity, analyze the business process, and set the goals of the system improvement.
- Discover the system requirements with requirement discovery. A system requirement states what the information system should do and what properties and quality it should hold.
- Analyze the studied problem with a standardized method. Pick one of the three recommended methods. PIECES, SWOT, or Information Characteristics.

## PIECES

- Analyze Performance by stating the performance the system needs to meet user needs. Ask what response time is acceptable and what throughput rate is acceptable.
- Analyze Information by stating the information that matters to users in content, timeline, accuracy, and format. Ask what the input and output are used for and when they must happen, what data must be stored, what the current information should look like, and what interface the external system needs.
- Analyze Economics by stating the economic needs of the system to cut costs or to raise profit. Ask which part of the system must cost less, how much cost must fall or profit must rise, what the budget limit is, and what the development timetable is.
- Analyze Control by stating the environment where the system must operate and the type and level of security to provide. Ask whether access to the system or the information must be controlled, what the privacy requirements are, and whether critical data needs special handling such as backup and off-site storage.
- Analyze Efficiency by stating the need for the system to produce output with minimal waste. Ask whether duplicated process steps must be removed.
- Analyze Service by stating the need for the system to stay reliable, flexible, and expandable. Ask who will use the system and where they sit, whether user types differ, which human factors apply, what training tools and materials belong in the system, what training tools must stay separate such as a stand-alone computer-based training program or database, what the reliability and availability requirements are, how the system will be packaged and distributed, and what documentation is needed.

## SWOT

- State the Strength as the strengths of the existing running system.
- State the Weakness as the weaknesses of the running system.
- State the Opportunity as the hopes and chances for improvement or a solution for the running system.
- State the Threat as the threats the running system will face.
- Build the SO strategy by using every strength to seize and exploit the opportunity as far as possible.
- Build the ST strategy by using the strengths of the company to overcome the threat.
- Build the WO strategy by exploiting the opportunity while minimizing the weakness.
- Build the WT strategy as a defensive move that minimizes the weakness and avoids the threat.

## Information Characteristics

- Check Relevansi by asking whether the information from data processing matches the needs of the decision makers.
- Check Keakuratan by asking whether the information is free of errors, because inaccurate information leads to wrong decisions and the truth of information depends on the validity of the source data.
- Check Ketepatan Waktu by asking whether lower management gets its repeating periodical reports and whether upper management can get information whenever it needs it.
- Check Ekonomi by asking whether the benefit of the information beats the cost of getting it.
- Check Efisiensi by asking whether the structure, format, and logical links of the information serve as a measure of its meaning, for example in the number of documents, pages, words, letters, and bits.
- Check Reliabilitas by asking whether the information stays dependable, meaning repeated measurement with a tested instrument gives the same data, and whether the measurement can trace back the validity of the source data.
- Check Kegunaan by asking whether lower management gets information from internal company data and whether upper management gets information from external data.

## Usulan Sistem

- Present the global design of the proposed system.
- Match the diagrams to the analysis and consult the supervisor on the choice.
- Describe the proposed system for Sistem Informasi with a document flowchart or flowmap for data procedures. Describe a computerized process with its input, output, and data store using a UML use case diagram, an activity diagram, and a wireframe design when the approach is structured.
- Describe the proposed system for Teknik Informatika networks with a proposed topology or a computer network architecture blueprint. Describe a proposed IoT system with a block diagram, a flowchart, a full IoT blueprint, the needed hardware and software, and the preparation for applying it. Describe a proposed AI system with the AI algorithm, a flowchart, and the design of the AI based application.
- Describe the proposed system for Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak mobile with a document flowchart or flowmap for data procedures, plus a UML use case diagram, an activity diagram, and a wireframe design. Describe a proposed game system with the same diagrams plus a storyboard.
- Describe the proposed system for Manajemen Informatika with a document flowchart or flowmap for data procedures, plus a UML use case diagram, an activity diagram, and a wireframe design for a computerized process.

## Simpulan

- State the important findings from the discussion as general or specific statements.
- Draw every conclusion from the earlier subchapters and the discussion results.

## Saran-saran

- Give development suggestions for the host organization or company.
- Cover the managerial aspect, the system aspect, and the further research aspect.
