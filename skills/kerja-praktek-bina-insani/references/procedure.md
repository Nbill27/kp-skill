# Procedure

## What Kerja Praktek is

- Treat the KP report as proof that the student carried out an activity independently at an institution or company.
- Cover the type of activity, the time of the activity, the literature used, and the work done in the field.
- Use KP to apply the theory and knowledge from class to real work and to widen the student view of the study field.
- Treat KP as a consequence of the curriculum for Sistem Informasi, Teknik Informatika, Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak, and Manajemen Informatika, with a weight of 4 SKS.

## Technical rules for the placement

- Run KP individually.
- Cover at least two things in the scope. Analyze the problems at the KP site and produce a recommendation or a proposed system improvement.
- Place the student for at least 1 month and at most 6 months at a government institution or a private company with legal status such as a PT, a CV, or another legal business, in a division that matches the study program.
- Keep the activity from disturbing the student study schedule.
- Write a proposal and a request letter to the host institution through BAA before the KP starts.
- Follow the host work in line with the study program field of knowledge.
- Base the report analysis on the work the student actually did at the host.
- Match the proposed system to the study program outline. Sistem Informasi focuses on business solution analysis and IT audit. Teknik Informatika focuses on computer network, IoT, security, or AI analysis. Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak focuses on mobile application and game programming analysis. Manajemen Informatika focuses on web and desktop system analysis.
- Hold at least 5 supervision meetings with the campus supervisor, online or offline.
- Ask the host for the assessment and the letter stating that the student completed the internship or KP. The letter must use the host letterhead and carry an official stamp and the original signature of an authorized official, plus the signature of the KP supervisor.
- Send the KP assessment form, the research letter, and the full KP report as a PDF with all attachments to BAA.
- Count MBKM programs as equivalent to KP when they run in semester 7. The listed programs are certified internships, KKN, and Kampus Mengajar.

## Ethics during KP

- Get permission from the owner of the practice site before starting.
- Choose a host with legal business status that the government recognizes as an official company or institution. Never place KP at an informal business such as a street vendor.
- Act politely, follow the host rules and work culture, take responsibility for every action, and protect the good name of the alma mater.
- Stay proportional in joining the office work. Remember that the student is only an intern, so the activity must not harm the host institution or company.
- Never forge the letter or the KP grade. Forging either one turns the grade into E and bars the student from joining the skripsi guidance process.
