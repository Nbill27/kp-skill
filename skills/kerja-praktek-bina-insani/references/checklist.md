# Final Check

- Run the check in four layers, in this order. Fix a layer before moving to the next one.

## Layer 1, substance

- Check the traceability map rumusan masalah to tujuan to bab to bukti to simpulan to saran. Every problem in the formulation must have a matching objective, a section that answers it, evidence that supports it, and a conclusion that answers it back. Report every orphan as a finding.
- Check that the outline matches the study program, including the Bab IV variant for Teknik Informatika.
- Check that Bab I through Bab V are complete and that every chapter follows the outline.
- Check that the background holds at least 1 journal citation showing the journal name and the ISSN or volume number.
- Check that the related research comes from ISSN journals within the last 5 years.
- Check that the literature review uses sources from the last 5 years.
- Check that 10 journal papers and 8 books or ebooks stand ready for the sidang.
- Check that no number in the text lacks a source and that every [ISI: ...] and [CEK: ...] marker is resolved.

## Layer 2, language

- Check the language follows EYD and KBBI and the guide language rules.
- Check that the five machine patterns are removed and that nothing was over-corrected.
- Check that the editing scope used matches what the user asked for, Koreksi, Sunting, Tulis ulang, or Tulis.

## Layer 3, format

- Check that the abstract exists in Indonesian and in English and ends with keywords.
- Check that the CV carries the photo in a white shirt and tie with the alma mater jacket against a red background.
- Check that the cover uses the study program color with silver text and that the title sits in capitals at size 14.
- Check that the paper is A4, the margins are top 4 cm, bottom 3 cm, left 4 cm, and right 3 cm, the font is Times New Roman 12, and the body uses double spacing.
- Check that the page numbering follows the three zones and that figures and tables are numbered by chapter.
- Check that every citation appears in Daftar Pustaka and that every Daftar Pustaka entry follows the guide format.

## Layer 4, integrity

- Check that the proposal and the request letter went through BAA before the KP started.
- Check that the host holds legal status and that the work division matches the study program.
- Check that the internship lasted at least 1 month and at most 6 months.
- Check that the student held at least 5 supervision meetings and that every meeting sits on the consultation card.
- Check that the daily log covers the whole period with dates, times, activities, mentor initials, and photos.
- Check that Surat Keterangan KP is the original on company letterhead showing the address and the telephone number, with an official stamp and an authorized signature.
- Check that the KP assessment form is the original with all 7 softskill scores, all 3 hardskill scores, the average score, and both signatures with the company stamp.
- Check that the full report and all attachments were sent to BAA as a PDF.
