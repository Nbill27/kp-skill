# Outlines

## Front matter

- Build the front matter in this order. Title page, approval page, preface, abstract in Indonesian, abstract in English, table of contents, list of symbols, list of figures, list of tables, and list of appendices.
- Check the title rule for KP. The KP guide sets the cover title format but sets no word limit for the KP title. Never apply the skripsi 14 word limit to a KP report.

## Bab I Pendahuluan

- Write Bab I with 1.1 Latar Belakang Masalah, 1.2 Identifikasi Masalah, 1.3 Batasan Masalah, 1.4 Perumusan Masalah, 1.5 Tujuan dan Manfaat Penulisan, and 1.6 Sistematika Penulisan.
- Use the wording Tujuan dan Manfaat Penelitian at 1.5 for Manajemen Informatika, which is a Diploma Three program. Use Tujuan dan Manfaat Penulisan at 1.5 for Sistem Informasi, Teknik Informatika, and Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak.

## Bab II Landasan Teori

- Write Bab II with 2.1 Tinjauan Pustaka and 2.2 Penelitian Terkait.
- Use the same Bab II structure for all four study programs.

## Bab III Objek Penelitian

- Write Bab III with 3.1 Sejarah Singkat dan Kegiatan Bisnis, 3.2 Struktur Organisasi Perusahaan, and 3.3 Wewenang dan Tanggung Jawab.
- Use the same Bab III structure for all four study programs.

## Bab IV by study program

- Write Bab IV for Sistem Informasi as Analisa Sistem Berjalan with 4.1 Tinjauan Umum Perusahaan, 4.2 Deskripsi Sistem, 4.3 Permasalahan Sistem, and 4.4 Usulan Sistem.
- Write Bab IV for Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak as Analisa Sistem Berjalan with 4.1 Tinjauan Umum Perusahaan, 4.2 Deskripsi Sistem, 4.3 Permasalahan Sistem, and 4.4 Usulan Sistem.
- Write Bab IV for Manajemen Informatika as Analisa Sistem Berjalan with 4.1 Tinjauan Umum Perusahaan, 4.2 Deskripsi Sistem, 4.3 Permasalahan Sistem, and 4.4 Usulan Sistem.
- Write Bab IV for Teknik Informatika as Analisa Jaringan or IoT or AI Berjalan with 4.1 Tinjauan Umum Jaringan or IoT or Sistem AI, 4.2 Deskripsi Jaringan or IoT or Sistem AI, 4.3 Permasalahan Jaringan or IoT or Sistem AI, and 4.4 Usulan Sistem.

## Bab V Simpulan dan Saran

- Write Bab V with 5.1 Simpulan and 5.2 Saran-saran.
- Use the same Bab V structure for all four study programs.

## Closing parts

- Close with Daftar Pustaka, Daftar Riwayat Hidup, Surat Keterangan KP, Nilai KP, and Lampiran-lampiran.
- Attach Surat Keterangan KP and Nilai KP whenever the student did KP at a company or institution. Both attachments must carry a stamp and a signature, and the letterhead must show the address and the telephone number.
- Accept only a host with legal status as the KP site. The list covers PT, CV, Firma, Perum, UD, PD, Koperasi, Lembaga Sosial such as a foundation or LSM, government institutions, education institutions, professional organizations, community or political organizations, and youth organizations, excluding student organizations.
