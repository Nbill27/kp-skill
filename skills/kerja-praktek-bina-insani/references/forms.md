# Forms and Attachments

## Title page

- Build the title page from the guide format. Put the KP title at the top, followed by the words LAPORAN KERJA PRAKTIK and the sentence starting with Diajukan untuk memenuhi mata kuliah Kerja Praktik.
- Put the student name and NPM in the middle and the study program, faculty, institution, city, and year at the bottom.
- Check the example title page in the guide for the exact layout.

## Approval page

- State the student name, NPM, study program, study program level, and KP title.
- State that the report was approved and accepted as part of the requirements for the Kerja Praktek course.
- Collect the signatures of the KP supervisor, the Dean of Fakultas Informatika dan Desain, and the Head of the Study Program, with the city and the approval date.

## Preface

- Thank God for the completion of the KP report and state the report title and the study program.
- Thank the people who guided and helped the student, in this order. Rektor, Wakil Rektor Bidang Akademik, Dekan, Ketua Program Studi, the KP supervisor, and the company director.
- Close with the hope that the report helps the readers and the note that criticism and constructive suggestions remain welcome.
- Type the preface at double spacing, date it, and sign it.

## Abstracts

- Write the abstract in Indonesian and in English.
- Open the Indonesian abstract with the student name and NPM on one line and the report title on the next line.
- End the abstract with keywords. The guide example uses 4 keywords.
- Check the abstract examples in the guide for the shape of the text.

## Lists

- Type the table of contents, the list of figures, the list of tables, and the list of appendices at single spacing.
- Number the figures by chapter in the list of figures, for example Gambar II.1 for the organization structure and Gambar III.1 for the proposed system diagram.
- Number the tables by chapter in the list of tables.
- Group the appendices by letter in the list of appendices. Use group A for screenshots and group B for program listings, for example Lampiran A.1 and Lampiran B.1.

## Daftar Riwayat Hidup

- Write the student biodata with NPM, full name, birthplace and birth date, and full address.
- List the formal education history with school names, places, and graduation years.
- List the non-formal education history such as courses and training with the institution and the year.
- List the organization and work experience with the organization name, place, position, and period.
- Date and sign the CV. Attach a color photo showing the student in a plain white shirt and a tie, wearing the alma mater jacket, against a red background.
- Type the CV at single spacing.

## Surat Keterangan KP

- Ask the host for a letter with a letter number and the subject of completing research or KP.
- Name the signer with the position held.
- Name the student with NPM and study program.
- State that the student truly did the research or KP at the company, with the start date and the end date, and that the student carried out the duties well and responsibly.
- Date the letter and sign it with the signer name and position.
- Put the letter on the host letterhead showing the address and the telephone number.
- Stamp the letter with the official company stamp.
- Accept only a host with legal status as the KP site. The list covers PT, CV, Firma, Perum, UD, PD, Koperasi, Lembaga Sosial such as a foundation or LSM, government institutions, education institutions, professional organizations, community or political organizations, and youth organizations, excluding student organizations.
- Treat the letter as mandatory. Attach it in the KP report.

## Form Penilaian KP

- Fill the header with the student name, NPM, class, university, study program, KP dates with a minimum of 1 month, company name, work unit, company address, telephone, and field supervisor.
- Score the 7 softskill components. Discipline and punctuality, attendance, attitude and work behavior, confidence and communication, teamwork and adaptation, initiative and responsibility, and neatness and appearance.
- Score the 3 hardskill components. Work ability, work skills, and work quality.
- Write the average score in the average field.
- Collect the approval signatures of the campus supervisor and the company assessor, with the assessor position and the company stamp.
- Attach the original form in the KP report together with Surat Keterangan KP.

## Daily log

- Fill one row per activity with the date, the time, the activity type, the KP work activity, the mentor initial, and a photo of the activity.
- Duplicate the sheet as needed to cover the whole internship period.
- Get the sheet acknowledged by the field supervisor with the name, the date, and the signature.

## Consultation card

- Record each meeting with the meeting number, the day and date with time, the discussion, the solution, the student initial, and the supervisor initial.
- Hold at least 5 meetings. Record every meeting on the card.
- Get the card acknowledged by the Head of the Study Program and the campus supervisor.
